# Future Vision

These ideas are intentionally not part of MVP unless promoted by an explicit decision.

## Operations
- Feed ingredient inventory.
- Feed formulation and batch costing.
- Feed consumption per flock.
- Water consumption.
- Cleaning/task tracking.
- Equipment and maintenance.

## Bird health
- Medication records.
- Vaccination schedules.
- Vitamins/supplements.
- Mortality events.
- Health observations.

## Commercial
- Customer management.
- Supplier management.
- Credit/outstanding balances.
- Payment tracking.
- Bird sales.
- Manure inventory and sales.

## Workforce
- Worker accounts.
- Task assignment.
- Attendance.
- Activity attribution.

## Analytics
- Production rate by flock and lifecycle stage.
- Rolling production averages.
- Cost per egg.
- Feed cost per bird/egg.
- Mortality rate.
- Forecasting and anomaly detection.

## Reporting
- Printable reports.
- CSV/Excel exports.
- Financial summaries.

## AI
Potential future uses:
- Detect unusual production changes.
- Explain trends.
- Forecast egg output.
- Highlight cost anomalies.

AI must assist interpretation, not silently alter source records.
