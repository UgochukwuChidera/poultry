# Project Blueprint

## Vision
Create one dependable place for farm records that are currently scattered across WhatsApp, notes, paper, memory, and ad-hoc entries.

The system should turn daily records into a usable operational picture: what happened, what is in stock, what was earned, what was spent, and how the farm is performing.

## Current farm context
- More than 1,000 birds.
- Two flocks currently.
- Eggs are sold in crates, with loose eggs also tracked.
- Customers include retailers and wholesalers.
- Egg price is generally fixed but can vary by customer.
- Regular costs include feed, feed-production materials, vaccines, drugs/medication, and other production materials.
- Workers participate in daily farm operations.
- The farm produces its own feed.
- Manure is produced and occasionally sold.

## Core product model
Farm → Flocks → Operational/financial events → Derived summaries.

## MVP
1. Farm/flock setup
2. Egg collection
3. Egg inventory
4. Egg sales/income
5. Expense recording
6. History
7. Dashboard

## Future-ready areas
- Feed production and ingredient inventory
- Feed consumption/cost allocation
- Medication and vaccination records
- Mortality
- Worker/task management
- Manure inventory and sales
- Bird sales
- Customer/supplier management
- Forecasting and production analytics
- Reports/export
- Multiple farms/users

## Explicit MVP non-goals
- Full accounting package
- Payroll
- Complex tax management
- Advanced forecasting
- Multi-farm administration
- Automated veterinary recommendations

## Key product principle
The application should not force the farm into an artificial model just because the software wants neat numbers. Real observations should be recorded, and trends/averages should be calculated from history.

For example, one unusually productive week should not become a permanent expected-production baseline.
